ASRA is the solution enabler between S-Bank's channels and its core systems.

Its scope covers four capability areas — and AI-assisted service runs through all of them. Step through to open each one.

  1. Case management & orchestration

    Case status, service level management (SLA), routing, auditing and traceability.

  2. Omnichannel contact center

    Voice, chat, email and messaging channels. Routing, queue management, recording, and the customer service agent desktop.

  3. Work engagement management

    Workforce scheduling, demand forecasting, quality management, performance management and coaching.

  4. Knowledge base

    Knowledge creation, maintenance and retrieval. Used by customer service representatives, agents, bots and service channels.

  5. AI-assisted service

    Self-service, AI assistants and voice bots, leveraging the bank's common AI platform. Guide · Assist · Automate.

Where boost.ai fits in the scope
  • AI-assisted service is boost.ai's core — AI agents for chat and voice that guide, assist and automate.
  • Knowledge base — answers are grounded in S-Bank's approved knowledge, shared with human agents.
  • Omnichannel contact center — the AI agent is the first line in chat and voice, with warm handoff into the contact center.
  • Case management — integrations let the agent create and update cases instead of just deflecting.
  • Work engagement — conversation and intent data inform forecasting; the WEM tool itself stays separate.

Today. S-Bank's reference architecture in three layers — channels on top, integrations in the middle, products and platforms underneath. Cybersecurity spans all of it.

Layer I · Channels & customer service. Many front doors — S-Mobile, chat, netbank, branches, call center — inside the wider S-Group and partner ecosystems.

Layer II · Process automation & integration. Process automation and agentic AI sit on top of a shared integration layer of APIs, MCPs and data integrations.

Layer III · Data, products & infrastructure. Core banking, wealth management, the data platform and the bank's common AI platform — plus S-Group common services.

Target. From channel-specific interactions to a shared customer view and workflow. ASRA is the solution-enabling layer between channels and core systems.

One view. Every channel reads from the same common customer view — interaction history, open activities, events, ownership and documents.

The ASRA layer. AI agents and automation that guide, assist and automate across five modules, governed by one security and governance model.

Integrations & core. ASRA consumes and produces data through the integration and service layer, on top of the core systems and bank-wide capabilities.

Why change. Customers repeat themselves, work is organised around steps rather than cases, and channels are managed in isolation.

The answer. One view of the customer, one pool of experts (phased) and one process orchestration — so the case progresses seamlessly across channels.

The payoff. Visible progress for customers, automated routine work for employees, and an operating model that scales — on one ASRA platform.
